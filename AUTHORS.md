# Authors

CoreTrace Compiler is developed within the [CoreTrace](https://github.com/CoreTrace)
organization.

## Maintainers

- Hugo Payet ([@SizzleUnrlsd](https://github.com/SizzleUnrlsd)): author of the
  project. Reviews and merges pull requests, publishes releases, and handles the
  reports described in [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Contributors

- Arthur Mazeau ([@Arthurvroum](https://github.com/Arthurvroum)): the first CI
  workflow, building on Linux, Windows and macOS
  ([#3](https://github.com/CoreTrace/coretrace-compiler/pull/3)).
- [@shookapic](https://github.com/shookapic): the Windows port of the compiler and
  of the instrumentation runtime
  ([#36](https://github.com/CoreTrace/coretrace-compiler/pull/36)), and thread-safe
  LLVM target initialisation
  ([#39](https://github.com/CoreTrace/coretrace-compiler/pull/39)).

Every change is recorded in the Git history: `git shortlog -sn` lists everyone who
authored a commit, and GitHub's
[contributors page](https://github.com/CoreTrace/coretrace-compiler/graphs/contributors)
shows the same. To contribute, see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
